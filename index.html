About the Studio

Interior Design With Character.

At Eros Interior, we create thoughtfully designed spaces that balance aesthetics, comfort and everyday functionality. From residential interiors to workspaces, every project is approached with attention to detail and a focus on creating spaces that feel personal.

We work on homes, offices and commercial spaces, and we carry the work from the first design conversation through to execution on site, so the finished room matches what was planned.

Process

From First Conversation to Handover.

A clear sequence, so you know what happens next at every stage of your project.

  1. Talk to us

    Share your space and what you need on WhatsApp, or visit us at Regent Market, Amtala.

  2. Visit & measure

    We see the space, take measurements and understand how it will be used.

  3. Design & materials

    Layout, design direction, finishes and lighting are worked out and shared with you for approval.

  4. Execution

    Carpentry, ceiling, lighting, finishes and furniture are carried out on site.

  5. Handover

    Final detailing and a walkthrough of your completed space together.
